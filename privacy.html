Privacy Policy

Tapvora Games · Last updated: November 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tapvora Games ("we", "us"), an independent game developer from Uzbekistan, handles information when you play our games. It applies to all our games, including:

  • Astro Dodge — Android app and browser game;
  • Silk Road Rumble — browser game;

and to our website tapvora.github.io. If a game has its own additional notice, it is shown inside that game.

1. Information we collect ourselves

We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or any account, and we do not run our own servers that store player data. Game progress (scores, coins, unlocked items, settings) is stored only on your device — in the app on Android, or in your browser's local storage in the web versions — and is never sent to us.

If you email us, we receive your email address and message and use them only to reply to you.

2. Advertising in Android apps

Our Android apps are free and show ads. Ads are delivered by Unity LevelPlay (ironSource, part of Unity) and its partner ad networks, including Unity Ads, the Yandex Advertising Network and Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). To show ads, limit repeated ads, measure performance and prevent fraud, these partners may automatically collect:

  • device identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID;
  • device information (model, operating system, language, screen size, network type);
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • ad interaction data (which ads were shown or tapped) and diagnostic or crash data.

On first launch the app asks whether you agree to personalized ads. If you decline, partners are told to show only non-personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time in the game's Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your Advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Consent (Google UMP). In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the app shows Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form, which follows the IAB TCF standard, before personalized ads are shown. In other regions, or if the form cannot be loaded, the app shows its own consent dialog. Your choice is passed to our ad partners.

Analytics (Yandex AppMetrica)

The Android app may use Yandex AppMetrica to understand how the app is used and to find crashes. AppMetrica may collect general app usage events, device and operating system information, device identifiers, IP address and crash data. We do not use it to identify you personally.

Partner privacy policies:

  • Unity / LevelPlay: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
  • ironSource: ironSource mobile privacy policy
  • Yandex (advertising network and AppMetrica): yandex.com/legal/confidential
  • Google: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

Google AdMob

When ads are served through Google AdMob, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares with Google, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention purposes:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate the general location of the device;
  • product interactions, such as app launches, taps and video views;
  • diagnostic information about app and SDK performance, such as app launch time, hang rate and energy usage;
  • device and account identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID, app set ID and, if applicable, identifiers related to accounts signed in on the device.

This data is encrypted in transit (TLS). You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your Android settings. Learn how Google uses this information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

3. Browser games

3.1 On our website (tapvora.github.io)

Browser games on our own website may show ads from these third-party networks:

  • Monetag — full-screen, in-page push and optional rewarded ads (monetag.com);
  • Adsterra — banner ads (adsterra.com).

These networks may use cookies and similar technologies, your IP address and basic browser and device information (browser type, language, approximate location) to show ads, limit how often the same ad appears, measure ad performance and prevent fraud. Depending on your region and settings, ads may be personalized. Rewarded ads are always optional. You can limit cookie-based tracking in your browser settings or by using private browsing mode.

3.2 Silk Road Rumble phone controller

If you use your phone as a controller in Silk Road Rumble, your phone and the game screen connect through a public message relay (MQTT) server using a temporary room code. We use public relay servers such as EMQX (broker.emqx.io), HiveMQ (broker.hivemq.com) and Eclipse Mosquitto (test.mosquitto.org), which are operated by third parties and may see your IP address and the messages sent. Mainly controller data (such as button presses and joystick movement) and the room code are sent. We do not intentionally send or store names, contacts or other personal data, and the room code is intended for temporary use only.

3.3 On game portals

When you play our games on a third-party portal (for example CrazyGames, Playgama, GameMonetize or Yandex Games), that portal operates the website, its accounts and any ads shown there, and handles data under its own privacy policy. We do not receive your personal data from the portals.

  • CrazyGames: crazygames.com/privacy-policy
  • Playgama: playgama.com
  • GameMonetize: gamemonetize.com
  • Yandex Games: yandex.com/legal/confidential

4. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell personal data and we do not share it with anyone except the advertising partners and portals described above, which process it under their own policies.

5. Children

Our games are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through an ad partner, contact us and we will help with the request.

6. Data security

Our website and games use encrypted connections (HTTPS) where available. Data collected by ad partners and portals is transmitted over encrypted connections and handled according to their own security practices.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, the EU/EEA, the UK, California or Uzbekistan), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Because we do not store player data ourselves, the fastest way to exercise these rights for ad data is through the ad partners and portals linked above — or contact us and we will help.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version, with its date, is always available on this page.

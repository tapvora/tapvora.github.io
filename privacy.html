Astro Dodge — Privacy Policy

Developer: Tapvora Games · Last updated: October 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Astro Dodge ("the Game"), published by Tapvora Games ("we", "us"), handles information when you play it.

1. Information we collect ourselves

We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or any account. Your progress (best score, coins, purchased ships, settings) is stored only on your device and is never sent to us.

2. Advertising

The Game is free and shows ads. Ads are delivered by Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) and its partner ad networks, such as Unity Ads. To show and measure ads, these partners may automatically collect:

  • device identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID;
  • device information (model, operating system, language, screen size);
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • ad interaction data (which ads were shown or tapped) and diagnostic or crash data.

On first launch the Game asks whether you agree to personalized ads. If you decline, partners are told to show only non-personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your Advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Partner privacy policies:

  • Unity / ironSource LevelPlay: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
  • ironSource: developers.is.com — privacy policy

3. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through an ad partner, contact us and we will help with the request.

4. Data security and retention

Data collected by ad partners is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and handled according to their own policies. Uninstalling the Game deletes all data stored on your device.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, the EU/EEA, the UK or California), you may have the right to access, delete or object to the processing of your data. Because we do not store your data ourselves, the fastest way to exercise these rights is through the ad partners linked above, or by contacting us.

6. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be available on this page.

7. Contact

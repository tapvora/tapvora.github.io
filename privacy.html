Astro Dodge — Privacy Policy

Developer: Tapvora Games · Last updated: October 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the game Astro Dodge ("the Game"), published by Tapvora Games ("we", "us"), handles information when you play it. The Game is available as an Android app and as a browser (web) game — on our own website tapvora.github.io and on third-party game portals.

1. Information we collect ourselves

We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or any account. Your progress (best score, coins, purchased ships, settings) is stored only on your device — in the app on Android, or in your browser's local storage in the web version — and is never sent to us.

2. Advertising in the Android app

The Android app is free and shows ads. Ads are delivered by Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) and its partner ad networks, such as Unity Ads and the Yandex Advertising Network. To show and measure ads, these partners may automatically collect:

  • device identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID;
  • device information (model, operating system, language, screen size);
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • ad interaction data (which ads were shown or tapped) and diagnostic or crash data.

On first launch the app asks whether you agree to personalized ads. If you decline, partners are told to show only non-personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your Advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Partner privacy policies:

  • Unity / ironSource LevelPlay: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
  • ironSource: developers.is.com — privacy policy
  • Yandex: yandex.com/legal/confidential

3. Web (browser) versions

3.1 On our website (tapvora.github.io)

The browser version on our own website may show ads from these third-party networks:

  • Monetag — full-screen ads and optional rewarded ads (see the privacy policy at monetag.com);
  • Adsterra — banner ads shown below the game area (see the privacy policy at adsterra.com).

These networks may use cookies and similar technologies, your IP address and basic browser and device information (browser type, language, approximate location) to show ads, limit how often the same ad appears, measure ad performance and prevent fraud. Depending on your region and settings, ads may be personalized. Rewarded ads are always optional — you choose whether to watch one in exchange for an in-game bonus. You can limit cookie-based tracking in your browser settings or by using private browsing mode.

3.2 On game portals (CrazyGames, Yandex Games and others)

When you play the Game on a third-party portal, that portal operates the website, its accounts and any ads shown there, and handles data under its own privacy policy:

  • CrazyGames: crazygames.com/privacy-policy
  • Yandex Games: yandex.com/legal/confidential

On Yandex Games, if you are signed in to your Yandex account, your game progress may be saved to that account by Yandex so you can continue on another device. We do not receive your personal data from the portals.

4. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through an ad partner, contact us and we will help with the request.

5. Data security and retention

Data collected by ad partners and portals is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and handled according to their own policies. Uninstalling the app, or clearing your browser's site data, deletes the game data stored on your device.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, the EU/EEA, the UK or California), you may have the right to access, delete or object to the processing of your data. Because we do not store your data ourselves, the fastest way to exercise these rights is through the ad partners and portals linked above, or by contacting us.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be available on this page.
